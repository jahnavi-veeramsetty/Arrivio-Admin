A.4Selected Payment Model

Principle: Arrivio never bills the same service twice in any model. Payment responsibility is clearly assigned to one party and shown separately.

A Employer pays in full

The Partner pays the full monthly usage fee directly to Arrivio. The resident has no direct payment relationship with Arrivio.

Tax treatment as a non-cash benefit (§ 8 EStG) is the Partner's responsibility. Arrivio provides no tax advice.

B Resident pays directly

The resident pays the usage fee directly to Arrivio. The Partner pays Arrivio a monthly service fee for the B2B portal and coordination.

C Mixed Model

The Partner pays a monthly housing subsidy per room; the resident pays the remainder directly. Both amounts are shown separately.
