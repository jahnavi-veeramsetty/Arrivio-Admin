Purpose of This Annex

Annex D establishes the data protection basis for the cooperation under GDPR and FDPA. It clarifies the roles of the parties, identifies data categories processed and the applicable legal bases, and contains the framework structure of the Data Processing Agreement (DPA) per Art. 28 GDPR.

D.1Roles under GDPR
RolePartyScope of Responsibility
ControllerArrivio GmbHResident data in the tenancy, billing, management, insurance organisation, portal operation
ControllerCooperation PartnerEmployee data within its own area of responsibility (HR management, portal use)
Data ProcessorArrivio GmbHWhere data is processed solely on the Partner's instructions → DPA per Art. 28 GDPR required (Section D.8)
The exact role classification must be verified and confirmed by a data protection lawyer in each specific case.

D.7Technical & Organisational Measures (TOMs)
  • ✓Role-based access and authorisation concept (least privilege)
  • ✓Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and where appropriate in storage
  • ✓Confidentiality obligations for all employees with access to personal data
  • ✓Regular data protection training
  • ✓Careful selection and contractual binding of all data processors
  • ✓Systematic deletion upon expiry of retention periods
  • ✓Documented data breach notification process per Art. 33/34 GDPR
D.8DPA Framework (Art. 28 GDPR)
Important: Framework ≠ Full DPA

This framework structure does not replace a fully elaborated DPA. Have it reviewed and drafted by a qualified data protection lawyer before final use.

ClauseContent
DPA-1: Subject matter & durationResidential letting/management for the term of the Cooperation Agreement
DPA-2: Arrivio's obligationsInstruction-bound processing, confidentiality, technical and organisational measures, support with data subject rights and breaches
DPA-3: Sub-processorsOnly with prior notification to Partner; own DPAs with sub-processors required
DPA-4: Data breachesPartner notification within 48 hours of becoming aware
DPA-5: Deletion after contract endDeletion or return of all data after contract end, unless statutory retention obligations apply
DPA-6: Audit rightsArrivio permits audits with minimum 4 weeks' prior written notice
⚠ Recommendations for Legal Review
  • Have full DPA per Art. 28 GDPR drafted by a qualified data protection lawyer
  • Confirm role classification (controller vs. processor) for each specific case
  • Conduct DPIA per Art. 35 GDPR for relevant processing activities
  • Check DPO obligation per Art. 37 GDPR for both parties
  • Review third-country transfer requirements (Art. 44 et seq. GDPR) if IT providers are outside the EU/EEA
