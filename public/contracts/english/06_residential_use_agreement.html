§ 1Subject Matter
(1)Arrivio rents the above fully furnished room to the Resident for private residential purposes only. This agreement is a sublease within the meaning of §§ 535 et seq. BGB. It is not a hotel, boarding house, or accommodation services contract.
(2)Arrivio als HauptmieterArrivio is the primary tenant of the property and is authorised to sublet. Should the primary lease end, Arrivio may terminate this sublease with 3 months' notice or offer the Resident an equivalent alternative property.
(3)EnergieausweisThe energy performance certificate per § 80 GEG was made available to the Resident before conclusion of this agreement.
§ 2Usage Fee & Base Package
(1)The usage fee is a total flat rate with no hidden charges. It includes: room use, operating costs, internet/WiFi, household contents insurance, private liability insurance, move-in coordination, landlord confirmation, ARIX assistant, and community platform.
(2)Optional additional services per Annex B are not part of the usage fee and are agreed and invoiced separately if required.

(1)Default interest accrues from the 11th working day after the due date per § 288(2) BGB.
(2)The Resident's right of set-off is limited to undisputed or legally established claims.
§ 4Security Deposit
(1)Deposit maximum 3 months' rent (§ 551 BGB analogously). Refund within 30 days of proper return and written confirmation of no damage. Planned deductions are communicated in writing with documentation; 14-day objection period.
§ 5Duration & Termination
(1)MindestlaufzeitMinimum term 12 months from move-in. Thereafter ordinary termination with 3 months' notice to the month-end (BGH VIII ZR 27/04).
(2)Kündigung ArrivioArrivio may terminate with immediate effect for: two months' arrears, serious house rule violations, unauthorised subletting, or loss of a valid residence permit.
(3)Upon termination of employment: notify Arrivio within 3 working days.
§ 6Insurance
(1)Household contents and liability insurance are included in the Base Package. Arrivio arranges cover via an insurance partner. Arrivio is not an insurer (§ 34d GewO). Coverage is governed exclusively by the respective insurer's terms.
(2)Damage must be reported to Arrivio and the insurer without delay. The Resident is obliged to cooperate in the claims process.
§ 7Internet & Digital Services
(1)Internet/WiFi (min. 50 Mbit/s) is included in the Base Package. No guarantee of uninterrupted service for provider outages. For private, non-commercial use only. Unlawful use of the connection is prohibited; the Resident is liable for such use.
§ 8Use & Resident Obligations
(1)NutzungszweckPrivate residential use only. Any commercial or other non-residential use is prohibited.
(2)SchadensmeldungDamage must be reported to Arrivio without delay, at the latest within 24 hours. Late reporting gives rise to liability for consequential damage.
(3)HausordnungThe House Rules (Annex C) are a binding part of this agreement.
(4)GästeDay visitors permitted at any time. Regular overnight guests require Arrivio's prior consent.
§ 9Maintenance & Repairs
(1)Minor repairs up to €100 per individual measure, maximum €300 per calendar year, are borne by the Resident. Repairs beyond this are arranged and borne by Arrivio.
(2)Maintenance measures by Arrivio must be tolerated by the Resident with 24 hours' advance notice.
§ 10Access by Arrivio
(1)Arrivio is entitled to access with 24 hours' notice for maintenance, viewings, and inspections.
(2)NotfallIn cases of imminent danger – in particular water, fire, or safety damage – immediate access without notice is permitted.
§ 11Subletting & Transfer of Use
(1)Subletting or otherwise transferring the room to third parties is expressly prohibited – including use via short-term rental platforms such as Airbnb.
(2)Violations entitle Arrivio to terminate with immediate effect and to claim damages.
§ 12Return of Accommodation
(1)The room must be returned swept clean, fully vacated, and with all keys. A return protocol is signed by both parties.
(2)Damage beyond normal wear and tear is charged on the basis of receipts. Normal wear and tear is not charged to the Resident.
(3)Verspätete RückgabeDaily compensation of 1.5× the daily rate plus €50 per working day of delay.
§ 13House Rules
(1)The House Rules (Annex C) are a binding part of this agreement. They contain provisions on quiet hours, cleanliness, waste sorting, guests, smoking, pets, internet use, and keys.
§ 14Partner Offers
(1)Use of partner offers via the Arrivio portal is voluntary. The respective third-party provider is the contracting party. The tenancy is entirely independent. Arrivio may receive a referral commission (disclosed herewith).
§ 15Residence Status & Registration
(1)The Resident confirms possession of a valid right of residence. Changes must be communicated to Arrivio without delay.
(2)§ 19 BMGArrivio issues the landlord confirmation per § 19 BMG for registration at the residents' registration office upon request.
(3)Loss of the right of residence may constitute good cause for extraordinary termination.
§ 16Data Protection
(1)Processing per Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR. Data protection contact: datenschutz@arrivio.global. Details per Annex D.
§ 17Liability
(1)Arrivio is fully liable for wilful misconduct, gross negligence, and personal injury. For slight negligence, liability is limited to the typically foreseeable damage.
(2)Liability per § 536a(1) BGB for defects existing at the time of conclusion of the agreement is limited to defects that Arrivio knew or ought to have known of.
§ 18Final Provisions
(1)SchriftformAmendments require written form. Oral side agreements are ineffective.
(2)Anwendbares Recht / GerichtsstandGerman law. Jurisdiction per § 29a ZPO is exclusively the local court at the location of the rental property.
