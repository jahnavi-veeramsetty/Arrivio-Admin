Note on Document Structure

This agreement governs the B2B framework relationship between Arrivio and the Cooperation Partner. Residential use relationships with residents are governed by separate Residential Use Agreements. Annexes A through D are binding parts of this agreement.

§ 1Subject Matter & Legal Classification
(1)Arrivio provides the Partner with fully furnished rooms in shared apartments (hereinafter: rooms) on the basis of this agreement. Provision is made within the framework of professional residential property management – not as a hotel, boarding house, or accommodation services arrangement.
(2)Arrivio als HauptmieterArrivio concludes primary or intermediate lease agreements with the respective property owners or tenants and acts as a professional residential property manager.
(3)WohnnutzungsvertragRooms are transferred to residents via separate Residential Use Agreements between Arrivio and the respective resident. The Partner is not party to this relationship.
(4)B2B-CharakterThis agreement establishes exclusively a B2B cooperation relationship covering contingent reservation, accommodation coordination, and management services. It is not a lease, tenancy, or accommodation services contract.
§ 2Definitions
(1)BewohnerNatural person using a room on the basis of a Residential Use Agreement with Arrivio and nominated by the Partner.
(2)KontingentThe minimum number of rooms agreed in Annex A that Arrivio reserves exclusively for the Partner.
(3)BasispaketThe standard services described in § 6 and Annex B that are included in the monthly usage fee.
(4)NutzungsgebührThe monthly flat-rate fee for room use, operating costs, and Base Package per Annex A.
(5)ServicepauschaleIndependent B2B remuneration for administration, coordination, and portal access, payable in Models B and C.
§ 3Term & Termination
(1)MindestlaufzeitThis agreement enters into force upon mutual signing and is concluded for an indefinite period. The minimum term is 24 months; ordinary termination is excluded during this period.
(2)Ordentliche KündigungAfter the minimum term, the agreement renews automatically by 12 months each time unless terminated with 3 months' notice to the renewal end date in text form.
(3)Außerordentliche KündigungEither party may terminate for good cause with immediate effect if material obligations remain unfulfilled despite a written warning and a 14-day cure period, or if insolvency proceedings are filed.
(4)EinzelbuchungenIndividual room bookings without a framework contingent have a minimum term of 6 months; thereafter terminable with 6 weeks' notice to the month-end.
§ 4Contingent & Availability
(1)The Partner undertakes to reserve the minimum contingent agreed in Annex A for the entire contract term. Arrivio holds these rooms exclusively available.
(2)ErhöhungThe contingent may be increased in writing at any time. Arrivio confirms within 5 working days subject to available capacity. There is no entitlement to immediate provision.
(3)ReduzierungReduction of the minimum contingent is possible in writing with 60 days' notice to the month-end. Shorter notice periods trigger cancellation fees per § 8.
§ 5Services Provided by Arrivio
(1)WohnraumFully furnished, move-in ready rooms including furnishings, initial bedding, shared kitchen, bathroom, operating costs, and Base Package per § 6.
(2)OnboardingFully digital booking and move-in process. No SCHUFA credit check or tenant deposit required from the resident.
(3)§ 19 BMGIssuance of the landlord confirmation per § 19 BMG for each resident for registration at the residents' registration office.
(4)B2B-PortalAccess to the Arrivio business portal with real-time occupancy overview, contract status, invoices, and booking management.
(5)VerwaltungOngoing residential management, communication with property owners, maintenance coordination, and point of contact for all housing matters.
(6)CommunityAccess to the Arrivio community platform with events, interest groups, and the ARIX assistant (24/7, multilingual).
(7)HR-SupportDedicated contact for the Partner's HR team on working days, response time maximum 1 working day.
§ 6Standard Services Included in the Base Package
(1)The following services are included in the monthly usage fee per Annex A and are provided for each resident within the contingent.
(2)Internet und WLANBroadband internet access (minimum 50 Mbit/s). Arrivio coordinates provision on a best-efforts basis but gives no guarantee of uninterrupted service where disruptions are caused by the provider. Disruptions must be reported without delay. Abusive use is prohibited.
(3)HausratversicherungArrivio arranges cover for each resident via selected insurance partners. Coverage is governed exclusively by the insurer's terms. Arrivio is not an insurer. Damage must be reported to Arrivio and the insurer without delay.
(4)Private HaftpflichtversicherungCover arranged via insurance partner. Arrivio does not act as an insurance broker within the meaning of § 34d GewO. The insurance partner's terms apply. Arrivio may receive remuneration for this.
(5)For insurance purposes, Arrivio requires resident data (name, date of birth, residential address, move-in date). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
§ 7Remuneration, Payment Models & Invoicing
(1)The usage fee is agreed in Annex A. All prices net plus statutory VAT. Arrivio never bills the same service twice in any model.
▸Model A: Partner pays the full usage fee directly. Tax treatment as non-cash benefit (§ 8 EStG) is the Partner's responsibility. Arrivio provides no tax advice.
▸Model B: Resident pays the usage fee directly. Partner pays a service fee for the B2B portal and coordination. The service fee is not a rent component.
▸Model C: Partner pays a housing subsidy; resident pays the remainder directly. Both amounts are shown and invoiced separately.
(2)RechnungsstellungMonthly, payable by the 10th of the month via SEPA transfer.
(3)VerzugszinsenDefault interest from the 11th working day after the due date per § 288(2) BGB.

§ 9Partner's Obligations
(1)Nomination of the resident (name, nationality, move-in date, planned length of stay) at least 5 working days before move-in in text form.
(2)Ensuring the resident has confirmed the House Rules (Annex C) in writing before move-in.
(3)Immediate notification of early departure, termination of employment, or other changes relevant to occupancy, at the latest within 3 working days.
(4)No independent communication with property owners, property managers, or tradespeople – this is exclusively Arrivio's responsibility.
(5)The Partner ensures that nominated residents are of legal age and not subject to any entry or residence ban.
§ 10Residential Use Relationship & Resident Obligations
(1)The resident concludes a separate Residential Use Agreement with Arrivio covering term, conditions of use, house rules, deposit, and return.
(2)NutzungszweckPrivate residential use only. Commercial, political, or other non-residential use is prohibited.
(3)SchadensmeldungDamage must be reported to Arrivio without delay, at the latest within 24 hours of discovery.
(4)VerboteSubletting, transfer to third parties, and structural alterations without consent are prohibited.
(5)ZutrittArrivio is entitled to access for maintenance with 24 hours' notice. In cases of imminent danger, immediate access without notice is permitted.
(6)RückgabeSwept clean, fully vacated, with all keys. Late return: 1.5× the daily rate plus €50 per working day of delay.
§ 11House Rules
(1)The full House Rules are Annex C and a binding part of this agreement. Each resident undertakes to confirm them in writing before move-in.
(2)RuhezeitenNight quiet daily 22:00–07:00, midday quiet 13:00–15:00, all-day quiet on Sundays and public holidays.
(3)RauchverbotAbsolute smoking ban in all indoor areas and communal spaces.
(4)HaustiereKeeping of pets only with Arrivio's prior written consent.
§ 12Security Deposit
(1)Arrivio charges a deposit per room per Annex A, maximum 3 months' rent analogously to § 551 BGB. Refund within 30 days of proper return, less any legitimate counterclaims.
(2)Planned deductions are communicated to the resident in writing with documentation. Objection period: 14 days.
§ 13Damage, Inventory & Return
(1)HaftungsmaßstabThe resident is liable for damage caused negligently. Normal wear and tear during contractual use is not charged to the resident.
(2)InventarInventory must be returned complete and in proper condition. Missing or damaged items are charged on the basis of receipts.
(3)The Partner is subsidiarily liable for resident damage where it failed to inform Arrivio without delay of known violations.
§ 14Liability
(1)Arrivio is fully liable for damage caused by wilful misconduct, gross negligence, and damage arising from injury to life, body, or health.
(2)For slight negligence, liability is limited to the typically foreseeable, contract-typical damage.
(3)Arrivio accepts no liability for services of independent third-party providers. The respective third-party provider is the contracting party.
§ 15Partner Offers, Third-Party Providers & Commission Disclosure
(1)Arrivio offers residents access to voluntary partner offers (sport, leisure, mobility, finance, everyday services). Use is voluntary; no entitlement to availability.
(2)The contracting party for third-party services is exclusively the respective third-party provider. The tenancy is entirely independent of the use of partner offers.
(3)Arrivio may receive a commission from the third-party provider upon successful referral. This has no effect on the price for the resident. Arrivio discloses this transparently herewith.
(4)RegulatorischArrivio is not an insurance broker (§ 34d GewO), not a financial investment intermediary (§ 34f GewO), and not a legal or tax adviser.
§ 16Data Protection
(1)Arrivio GmbH is the controller for data processed in connection with this agreement. The Partner is responsible for data collection within its own domain.
(2)Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract performance), (c) (legal obligation), (f) (legitimate interest for security and debt recovery).
(3)A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) per Art. 28 GDPR is to be concluded per Annex D where Arrivio processes data on the Partner's behalf. Data protection contact: datenschutz@arrivio.global
§ 17Tax Notes
(1)All prices net plus applicable statutory VAT at the time of invoicing. Arrivio issues tax-compliant invoices with a separate VAT statement.
(2)Arrivio provides no tax advisory services. Questions regarding the treatment of non-cash benefits (§ 8 EStG) or tax implications are for the Partner to clarify independently with a tax adviser.
§ 18Confidentiality
(1)Both parties undertake to treat all confidential information obtained in connection with this agreement – in particular price terms, contingents, client data, and technical details – as strictly confidential and not to disclose it to third parties without the other party's consent.
(2)This obligation applies for 3 years beyond the end of the agreement.

(1)SchriftformAmendments require written form. Oral side agreements are ineffective.
(2)Salvatorische KlauselInvalid provisions are replaced by a legally valid provision most closely achieving the economic purpose.
(3)Anwendbares RechtGerman law, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG).
(4)GerichtsstandExclusive place of jurisdiction is Arrivio's registered seat, provided both parties are merchants within the meaning of the HGB.
