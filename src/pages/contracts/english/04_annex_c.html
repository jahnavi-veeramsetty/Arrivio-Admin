Purpose of These House Rules

These House Rules form the basis for respectful cohabitation. They are a binding part of the Residential Use Agreement and the Cooperation Agreement. Each resident undertakes to comply upon signing. Violations may result in a written warning, cost charges, or termination of the agreement.

1Room, Inventory & Accommodation
  • ✓Handle room, furniture, appliances, and communal areas with care
  • ✓Report damage without delay – at the latest within 24 hours of discovery
  • ✓Ventilate regularly to prevent mould growth
  • ✗Damage to floors, walls, doors, windows, or plumbing installations
  • ✗Structural alterations, drilling, or installations without written consent
  • ✗Tampering with, covering, or deactivating smoke detectors
Normal wear and tear during contractual use is not charged to the resident.
2Cleanliness & Hygiene
  • ✓Clean private room regularly and keep it in a clean state
  • ✓Leave kitchen, bathroom, and corridor clean after every use
  • ✓Remove food residue and grease immediately after use
  • !For persistent violations: Arrivio may commission professional cleaning at cost to those responsible

5Guests & Visitors
  • ✓Day visitors are permitted at any time, provided no disruption to fellow residents
  • ✓Occasional overnight guests welcome (up to 14 days without notification requirement)
  • ✗Stays exceeding 14 days: notify Arrivio in text form in advance
  • ✗Permanent accommodation of additional persons without Arrivio's written consent
  • !Resident is liable for damage and violations caused by their guests
6Prohibited Uses
  • ✗Subletting or transferring the room – including via platforms such as Airbnb
  • ✗Sharing keys or access codes with third parties
  • ✗Commercial, political, or other non-residential use
  • ✗Illegal substances or dangerous items
  • !Pets only with Arrivio's prior written consent
  • !Own furniture replacing Arrivio inventory only with consent
7Smoking & Fire Safety
  • ✗Smoking in all indoor areas and communal spaces – including e-cigarettes
  • ✗Storage of flammable, explosive, or otherwise dangerous items
  • ✓Always keep escape routes and emergency exits clear
8Internet & WiFi
  • ✓For private, non-commercial use only
  • ✓Report disruptions to Arrivio without delay
  • ✗Illegal downloads, file-sharing, cyberattacks, or Darknet access
  • ✗Sharing the access code – not even with temporary guests
  • !The resident is liable for unlawful use of the internet connection
9Insurance & Damage Reporting
  • ✓Contents and liability insurance in Base Package – Arrivio arranges cover
  • ✓Report damage to Arrivio and the insurer without delay
  • ✓Duty to cooperate in the claims process with the insurer
  • !Arrivio is not an insurer – coverage is governed by the insurer's terms
10Keys & Access Means
  • ✓Keep keys and access codes safe
  • ✓Report loss or theft to Arrivio immediately
  • ✓Return all keys and access means in full upon move-out
  • ✗Pass keys to third parties or have them duplicated
  • !Replacement key and any lock change costs are borne by the resident
11Move-out & Return
  • ✓Return room swept clean and fully vacated
  • ✓Hand over inventory complete and in proper condition
  • ✓Sign handover/return protocol jointly with Arrivio
  • !Damage, professional cleaning, and missing items are documented and charged
